## Currently working on

**Verifiable code generation in Rust with Verus** — research intern in the
[UIUC++ SRSE](https://uiuc-srse.github.io/) program, mentored by
[Prof. Darko Marinov](https://mir.cs.illinois.edu/marinov/) (UIUC) and
[Prof. Wenxi Wang](https://wenxiwang.github.io/) (UVA). Specification testing and automated
test-case generation for the [VeriContest](https://hiprel-group.github.io/VeriContest/) benchmark:
finding formal specifications that are under- or over-constrained relative to the problem, yet still
verify against their own implementation.

**LLM-based building-code compliance checking** — research assistant at the BUET CSE Incubator under
[Prof. Anindya Iqbal](https://cse.buet.ac.bd/faculty/faculty_detail/anindyaiqbal). An agent that
compiles building-code clauses into executable checkers over IFC models, and manufactures its own
ground truth by perturbing models into fixtures with known verdicts rather than asking an LLM to
grade itself.
